# SuperbAddon Wiki

SuperbAddon is an addon for SuperbWarfare on Minecraft Forge 1.20.1.

This wiki documents installation, configuration, content trimming, shell ejection rules, knockback rules, and vehicle recipe overrides.

## Supported Environment

- Minecraft: 1.20.1
- Mod Loader: Forge 47.4.0
- Java: 17
- Dependency: SuperbWarfare
